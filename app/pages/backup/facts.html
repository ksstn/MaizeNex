Quick Corn Facts

Short, practical notes for farmers, cooks, and curious readers.

Origin

Maize was domesticated in Mesoamerica and spread globally through trade and agriculture.

Climate

Most corn varieties prefer warm, sunny weather with consistent moisture.

Pollination

Wind carries pollen from tassels to silks, so spacing matters for strong yields.

Soil

Well-drained loam with plenty of organic matter supports healthy root growth.

Harvest

Sweet corn is harvested earlier, while dent corn stays longer for drying.

Uses

Corn becomes flour, feed, starch, syrup, biofuel, and countless food products.

Kernel Types at a Glance

Each type has a distinct texture and best use. Knowing the difference helps with planting and marketing.

  • Flint: hard shell, strong storage, common in traditional foods.
  • Dent: soft starch core, used for feed and industrial products.
  • Sweet: high sugar, picked early for fresh eating.

Growth Timeline (Typical)

Timing varies by variety and climate, but the stages follow a clear rhythm.

Week 1-2

Germination and early leaf growth.

Week 4-6

Rapid stalk growth and leaf expansion.

Week 7-9

Tasseling and silking for pollination.

Week 10+

Kernel fill and harvest readiness.
